# Handoff: first dynamic flight tests (10 Oct 2026)

**Read this first next session.** It covers what the four flights showed, what was changed on the
Jetson afterwards, and, most importantly, **what to do next, in order**.

---

## TL;DR

- **Four automatic GPS missions flown and recorded cleanly** (camera looking down, 5–9 m high, up to
  10 m/s, 1.2 km in total). Every flight is paired with its flight-controller log and analysed.
- **OpenVINS is not flight-ready yet.** Orientation and early scale are right, but position drifts:
  **about 10 m off after 50–100 m** in every flight. Worst case: 390 m off (flight 1).
- **Two conditions drive the drift:** (1) **ground moving too fast in the image** (flying fast and
  low), and (2) **plain ground with little texture**, which causes drift even while hovering.
- **OpenVINS stopped producing output mid-flight in 3 of 4 flights.** Cause unknown; logging and
  crash dumps are now in place to catch it.
- **Jetson fixes made afterwards (committed on the Jetson, NOT pushed):** clock no longer resets to
  1970, a one-command flight routine, crash dumps, a clean OpenVINS shutdown, and `check_setup.sh` fixes.
- **Next:** bench-test the new scripts with the camera → replay the recorded flights offline to try
  fixes → cheap hardware fixes → calibrate on the final mount → step-by-step flight tests.
- **Going out to fly?** Use the **"NEXT FLIGHT DAY CHECKLIST"** section near the end.

## Conclusions

1. **The recording and analysis pipeline works.** All bags are complete (30 Hz stereo IR, 493 Hz
   IMU, no gaps) and can be replayed offline. Bag ↔ flight-controller log are time-aligned by
   gyro cross-correlation (r > 0.99). The camera is mounted **looking down, tilted ~7°** (from the gyro fit).
2. **What OpenVINS gets right:** orientation (tilt ≤ 4°, heading ≤ 15°) and early scale (0.85–1.09).
   The calibration is not grossly wrong, unlike the old 90° bug.
3. **What goes wrong:** velocity, then position, then height drift. Typically 10 m off after 50–100 m.
   Height is the weakest axis.
4. **Maximum drift = image motion too large** (speed ÷ height too high). Flight 1 (4.7 m, 9–10 m/s):
   features moved **~40 px between frames, about 10× the other flights**; tracking success dropped to
   ~80 % (the only time it dropped). The ground there was *well* textured, so motion is the cause.
5. **Second cause = low-texture ground.** Windows with < 300 FAST corners/image or low sharpness drift
   ~2.5× faster (median 0.82 vs 0.31 m/s), **even when hovering**. Flight 3 crossed the plainest ground.
6. **Minimum drift:** ~9 m height, ≤ 3 m/s, little yawing, ground with tracks or objects in view.
7. **Rule of thumb (provisional):** every good window had speed ÷ height ≤ **~0.35 s⁻¹**, i.e. about
   ≤ 1.7 m/s at 5 m, ≤ 3 m/s at 9 m, ≤ 5 m/s at 15 m. Based on few windows; confirm by flying.
8. **No clear effect:** camera vibration at 9 m (the camera IMU shakes 1.5–2× more than the FC's),
   acceleration, image brightness (no saturation), compute (output latency stayed 40–75 ms, no backlog).
9. **OpenVINS is badly over-confident** (5–10 % of errors within its own 3σ, against ~99 % for a
   consistent filter). The `vps_bridge` sanity filter relies on that covariance, so **it would not
   reject these errors**. Likely cause: guessed IMU noise parameters, no Allan variance test, no proper calibration.
10. **Flight 2 is not special.** It looks best because OpenVINS stopped after its easiest stretch
    (straight, slow). At equal distance it is only somewhat better than the others.
11. **OpenVINS stopping mid-flight (flights 2, 3, 4): cause unknown.** Output stopped abruptly with
    normal latency (not overload). Two hypotheses:
    (a) its terminal output blocked when the SSH/Wi-Fi link dropped (flights 2 and 3 stopped at their farthest points);
    (b) a genuine crash: Jetson launch logs show spontaneous segfaults (exit −11) in some runs, but they
    could not be matched to flights because every boot logged in "1970".
    Flight 4 stopped near the ground station, which argues against (a) alone.

## NEXT FLIGHT DAY CHECKLIST

Agreed after the analysis. The flight conditions come from the team's plan; the notes say what the data supports.

### Flight conditions

| Plan | Note from the data |
|---|---|
| **Avoid direct sunlight** (overcast or diffuse light) | Brightness had no measurable effect and nothing was over-exposed. The real benefit is that direct sun casts the **drone's own sharp shadow**, which moves with the drone and can confuse tracking. Avoid dusk-level darkness. |
| **Featureful ground** (tracks, grass edges, objects) | Strongly supported: low-texture ground drifted ~2.5× faster, even while hovering. |
| **Straight out-and-back legs, few turns** | Supported: the straightest stretch (flight 2) drifted least. Return to the take-off mark every time. |
| **Higher altitude, slower speed** | Strongly supported. Keep **speed ÷ height ≤ ~0.35 s⁻¹** (≤ 3 m/s at 9 m, ≤ 5 m/s at 15 m). Provisional; this test day should confirm it. |
| **Lidar for altitude** | OpenVINS cannot use lidar directly. It gives an accurate **height reference** for judging OpenVINS now, and could feed the flight controller later. |
| **Higher camera frame rate** | First make OpenVINS **use every frame it already gets** (it appears to process ~15 of 30 Hz; confirm in `estimator_config.yaml`). That is free and testable on the recorded bags. Raise the camera's own fps only if that is not enough. |
| **Tilt the camera 15–30° forward** | Reasonable start (30–45° if needed). The IMU is inside the camera, so tilting the whole camera **does not change T_cam_imu**; only the bracket changes. |

## Gotchas

- `export ROS_LOCALHOST_ONLY=1` is mandatory on vajra (7 network interfaces break DDS discovery).
  The `flight_*` scripts set it.
- Full power is `nvpmodel -m 2` (`MAXN_SUPER`); `-m 0` is the throttled 15 W mode. `jetson_clocks` is needed
  after every boot (`flight_start` runs it).
- After any power-on, the Jetson clock is the *last saved* time (behind by the downtime) until
  `sync_jetson_clock.sh` is run. `flight_start` warns about this.
- OpenVINS needs motion to initialise: lift and slide the drone gently before recording.
- Wi-Fi: `wifi-mode flight` (`MOTHER_DRONE` AP, 10.42.0.1) / `wifi-mode internet` (joins TU). SSH over
  USB (192.168.55.1) works in both modes.
